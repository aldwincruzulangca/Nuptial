Dress Code

Guest Dress Code

Ladies

Formal dresses: floor-length, pastel colours, or fun summer colours. Patterns are encouraged.
Please avoid wearing: any shades of white, baby blue (bridesmaids colour) and black.

Gentlemen

Please opt for a coloured suit, like tan, sage or pastel shades, paired with a crisp white dress shirt. Black suit if other colours are not available. Breathable fabrics like linen or cotton are recommended for comfort. A tie is optional, but encouraged.
Please avoid wearing: sneakers, jeans, short-sleeve polos, and a navy coloured suit as it is reserved for the groom.

FAQ

Are children welcome?

While we love your children, this is an adults-only event. The only children attending will be those in the wedding party. We appreciate your understanding!

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to our venue capacity, we can only accommodate guests named on your RSVP. We will inform you if you are receiving a plus one. There will be no exceptions. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

What should I do if I cannot make it?

We will miss you! If you are unable to attend, please select "Decline" on your RSVP so we can plan accordingly. If you've already RSVP'd "Accept" but your plans have changed, please reach out to our wedding coordinator, Jam/David, as soon as you can.

What should I wear to the wedding?

Dress to impress! We request a strictly formal attire, meaning ladies should wear floor length dresses or gowns and men should wear suits. For outfit inspiration, check out the Dress Code card. A friendly reminder that casual attire such as jeans, t-shirts, flip-flops, and sneakers are not suitable for this event. For the ladies please avoid anything in the shades of white, maroon, or black. Think fun summery colours!

Will there be a bar at the reception?

Yes! Drinks are on us, but the hangover is on you!

What happens if I miss the deadline?

Unfortunately, we won't be able to accommodate RSVPs after the deadline as we need to provide final numbers to our venue and caterer. If we don't receive your RSVP by the deadline of April 8th, you will be marked as "Not Attending". We appreciate your understanding but no exceptions can be made.

Gifts

With all that we have, we are truly blessed. Your presence and prayers are all that we need. But, if you desire to give nonetheless, a monetary gift is one we suggest.
